## Hi there 👋

Hi, I’m a Computer Science student in the Honors College at the University of Illinois Chicago. A lot of what I enjoy about computer science comes from something I discovered long before I started studying it seriously: I really like solving hard problems. 🔥

In high school, I was heavily involved in competitive mathematics. I spent years on my school’s math team, eventually becoming captain, competing at regional and state competitions, and spending a lot of time helping other students prepare. What I loved most was not just getting the answer, but the process of staring at a problem that seemed impossible, trying different ideas, finding a pattern, and finally having everything click.

That is also what pulled me toward programming. Coding and LeetCode problems give me the same thrill and access the same part of my mind that competitive math did. I like having to figure out how a problem works, come up with an approach, test it, realize where I went wrong, and keep working until I find a better solution. I’m especially interested in software engineering because I enjoy taking that problem-solving mindset and turning it into something practical. Right now at UIC, I’m building that foundation through coursework in C, C++, discrete mathematics, applied linear algebra, and technical interview preparation, while continuing to practice programming outside of class. 

Outside of academics and programming, I’m pretty competitive in other parts of my life too. I’ve played soccer for years and was a varsity co-captain in high school, I enjoy playing basketball, and I’ve recently started taking working out more seriously. I like having things to work toward, whether that means solving a problem I’ve been stuck on, getting better at a programming concept, improving in the gym, or becoming a better athlete. That same mindset is a big part of how I approach school and my career. I’m hoping to keep challenging myself, gain real software engineering experience, and eventually work on problems where I can combine the analytical side of mathematics with the creativity and practicality of computer science.
